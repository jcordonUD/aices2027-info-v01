# AICES 2027: practical information page (prototype v01, classic design)

Test prototype of the practical information page (travel, venue, hotels, Bilbao) for AICES 2027, the 2nd International School on Human-AI Collaboration in the Era of Agentic AI, Universidad de Deusto, Bilbao, 4 to 7 May 2027.

**This is a test prototype, not the official website of the school.** The official site is https://aices.irdta.eu/2027/ and the organisers will decide what is published there. The page carries a `noindex, nofollow` robots tag so that search engines do not index it.

Generated on 18 November 2026.

## Contents

- `index.html`: standalone page with the AICES corporate look (logo, green #009434, photos of Bilbao). All images are embedded in base64; only Google Fonts are loaded from the internet.
- `.nojekyll`: tells GitHub Pages to serve the HTML as is.

## Notes on the data

- Walking distances to the campus are estimates (straight-line distance multiplied by 1.3) and are indicative only.
- Hotel data, rates and timetables were checked in November 2026.
